Founder Origin Story

After blown head gaskets and hidden engine damage cost me thousands on used car purchases, I realized existing tools were completely inadequate. Diagnostic OBD2 code readers only report errors after sensors fail — they can't evaluate the physical lifeblood of the engine.

We built DipCheck to bring laboratory-grade spectroscopic analysis directly to every car owner's pocket. By pairing mobile optics with high-dimensional computer vision models, DipCheck sees the unseen wear before expensive breakdowns occur.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does DipCheck analyze oil without physical lab equipment?+
DipCheck uses calibrated multi-spectral optical analysis through your smartphone camera. By observing how light refracts and absorbs through oil sample droplets on our target card, our machine learning models match fluid degradation signatures calibrated against lab mass spectrometry data.
Do I need special hardware or an OBD2 adapter?+
No OBD2 dongle or extra hardware is required. DipCheck works entirely using your phone's camera and our sample calibration target card.
What types of fluids can DipCheck analyze?+
DipCheck currently analyzes gasoline and diesel engine oils, with support for automatic transmission fluid (ATF), engine coolant, and brake fluid releasing in Phase 2.
Is DipCheck accurate for evaluating pre-owned vehicles?+
Yes! Our 48h Weekend Pack is built specifically for pre-purchase inspections, instantly detecting hidden head gasket leaks, severe fuel dilution, or heavy metal wear before you sign a bill of sale.
